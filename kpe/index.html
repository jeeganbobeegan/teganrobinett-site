Tegan Robinett

Composer, songwriter and vocalist making contemporary art pop.

Tegan Robinett is a composer, songwriter and vocalist who splits her time between Berlin and New York City. Her background is in classical voice study at the Thornton School of Music at the University of Southern California, and the Colburn Conservatory's Orchestra da Camera. She now makes contemporary art pop, composing on synth and piano with layered vocals, percussive elements and ambient sound.

Her debut album otherworld came out in February 2026. Partnered with Brazilian producer Leonardo Tows, she took inspiration from Tír na nÓg, the Otherworld of Celtic myth.

Her latest single, hot air balloon, followed in July, heralding a new era for the artist. She's excited to share her EP Flight later this year.

For fans of Björk, Joni Mitchell, Imogen Heap, Elizabeth Fraser, Linda Perhacs.
